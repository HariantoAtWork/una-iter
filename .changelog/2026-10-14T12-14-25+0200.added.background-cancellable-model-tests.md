# Background model-test queue with cancel and SSE

Model tests used to block the drawer, and a single per-provider slot made it hard to
reason about parallel work the way dns01-proxy’s cert queue does.

Model testing is now a real in-memory queue: each run gets a monotonic job id, sits in
`waiting` or `running` (at most one active job per provider, many providers in parallel),
and lands in a short `recent` history when finished. Results are persisted per model as
they complete; cancel works by job id (or the provider’s active job) without discarding
already-tested models. The drawer reconnects over SSE after close and shows job
progress (`#id`, queued vs running).
